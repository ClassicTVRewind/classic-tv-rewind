Trivia

A quick classic-TV quiz for fans. Open each question to reveal the answer.

Which network first broadcast I Love Lucy?

CBS.

Which actor played Ethel Mertz?

Vivian Vance.

Who played Ricky Ricardo?

Desi Arnaz.

What was the Ricardos' landlords' surname?

Mertz.

Were the two lead actors married to each other off screen?

Yes, Lucille Ball and Desi Arnaz were married.
