About Us

Classic TV Rewind is an independent editorial publication exploring classic American television, beginning with I Love Lucy. We publish original articles, character studies, television-history explainers and trivia for readers who enjoy looking beyond the screen.

What we do

Our goal is to make classic television approachable for longtime fans and curious new viewers. We focus on clear writing, useful context and thoughtful commentary rather than reproducing episodes, clips or promotional material.

Independent and unaffiliated

Classic TV Rewind is a fan-run editorial project. It is not owned by, sponsored by, or affiliated with CBS, any television network, studio, performer, or rights holder. All show titles and character names belong to their respective owners.

Our editorial standards

We aim to distinguish documented history from our own interpretation, correct mistakes when they are brought to our attention, and avoid using material we do not have permission to publish. Read our Editorial Policy and Copyright Policy for more details.

Questions or corrections? Visit our Contact page.
